# Sandbox rules

This sandbox is practice only. It is not assessed, so mistakes here cost nothing.

## Session 0: checking in

1. **Comment on the pinned [Session 0 check-in](https://github.com/drweihungpan-lab/3R-sandbox/issues/1) issue.** Do not open a new issue.
2. **Paste only the output of the three check commands** from the [Session 0 guide](https://github.com/drweihungpan-lab/3R-teaching/blob/main/docs/session-0.md#step-7-check-in): `git --version`, `python3.12 --version` and `ssh -T git@github.com`.
3. **Never paste private keys, passwords or tokens.** Not in a comment, not anywhere in this repository.
4. **Everything here is public.** Share nothing personal beyond your GitHub username.
5. **Edit your comment instead of reposting.** If you run the check again, edit your own comment so the issue stays readable.
6. **Deadline: 3 days before Session 1.** Your instructor will send the exact date.
7. **Do not push or change any files in Session 0.** You will start pushing in Session 1.
8. **Ask for help by replying under your own check-in comment.** Describe what you see; do not paste secrets.
9. **Be respectful.** This is a shared space for beginners. Be kind in everything you write.
10. **This is practice only.** It is not assessed.

## Session 1

1. **Work in the [practice folder](practice/README.md).** Do not edit other files unless the instructor says so.
2. **Name your branch `group-N/short-topic`**, where `N` is your group number, for example `group-3/add-notes`.
3. **You cannot push to `main`.** Open a pull request and have a teammate review it before merging.
4. **A review needs a specific comment** about behaviour or clarity. "Looks good" alone is not a review.
5. **Merge conflicts are expected.** The line under "Favourite tool this week" in `shared-notes.md` is shared on purpose.
6. **Nothing here counts as final-project evidence.** Your group's own repository, created in Session 2, is where authored pull requests and reviews are counted.
7. **Never commit secrets, real data or personal information.** Everything here is public.
8. **Delete your branch after merging.**
